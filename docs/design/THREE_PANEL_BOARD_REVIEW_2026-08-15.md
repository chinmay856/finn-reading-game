  retain `USER FACTS ARE ALWAYS RIGHT` and `JUST TRUST ME`.
- ThreadIt's first panel deliberately compresses the visual logic into original,
  repeated, and independent/context groups; a production board should not add a dense
  live graph.

  zooms outward. The peer is fictional and the joy remains genuine.

  contradictions, AI-upscaled background, and bogus verification imagery carry the
  impersonation lesson.
- ViewTube assigns the advertising motive to the AI status display, not to Chinmay's

  the secured preview makes no purchase for Finn.
- Spotty-Fi preserves a persistent player and detailed current-song card while the
  center discovery surface tests creator visibility and attribution.
- MapGuess changes the route in Phase 1 and the destination itself in Phase 2, keeping
