# Reading-mission review synthesis — 2026-08-14

Status: **simulated review complete; revisions applied; not real user research**

This review tested the current right-rail reading missions rather than the older
left-side investigation concepts. It used a cautious teen lens, a confident teen
lens, two parent lenses with different risk sensitivities, and an implementation-
scope review. These simulations are design criticism, not evidence about real players.

## Converged findings

- The Reading Companion can carry the gameplay; the site canvas works as a layered
  visual reward and does not need to become a complex puzzle.
- Eight to twelve passages is defensible only with a pacing target. Most passages
  should initially target roughly 45–90 seconds aloud, with a provisional 12–18
  minute mission target subject to engine and reader testing.
- The midpoint cutscene belongs between passages and consumes no reading slot.
  Re-corruption occurs in that cutscene; the next passage must create a real visual
  change.
- Amy can clarify every mission and Chinmay can usually trigger Phase 2, but their
  wording, timing, and visual gag must vary so the ten missions do not feel cloned.
- Internal concepts such as provenance, authorization, context, and uncertainty
  need concrete teen-facing labels in the final copy.
- Reading feedback stays private and noncompetitive. Speed is descriptive, not
  inherently better. A failed recording never becomes a story penalty.

## Revisions applied

- Search-ish was shortened to ten passages and reframed around one coherent dossier.
- Amaze-On was shortened to eleven passages, narrowed to claims, durability, and
  waste, and given several responsible outcomes without fake player agency.
- Spotty-Fi now uses cultural artifacts as well as records, distinguishes consensual
  AI assistance from deceptive displacement, and does not ask Finn to detect a copied
  voice by ear.
- MyCorner includes trusted-adult escalation and avoids blame, risky contact, or
  real-person investigation.
- ThreadIt no longer implies that an official source is automatically true; it asks
  for the best independent information for the specific claim.
- FacePlace uses a human context sequence without diagnosing anyone's feelings.
- MapGuess is explicitly pre-trip planning and never asks for interaction while
  walking, cycling, or driving.
- Every mission's first post-midpoint passage now changes the site canvas.

## Readiness

All ten missions are ready for Chinmay's storyline review. No broad simulated teen,
parent, or implementation blocker remains. Passage authorship still requires factual
sourcing, reading-level checks, privacy/accessibility validation, and tests with real
readers before production freeze.
