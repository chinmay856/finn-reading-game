All three use the two-window desktop shell: WikiWhy and Reading Companion are
independent windows; Internet Recovery OS is represented by the desktop and taskbar.
Amy and Chinmay are absent from ordinary reading screens and appear only in temporary
dialogue overlays.

## Three distinct states

### Initial corruption

- clearly wrong dog-color claim;
- `USER FACTS ARE ALWAYS RIGHT` and `100% TRUE — NO QUESTIONS`;
- grayscale article image, missing spectrum graphic, missing citations, disabled
  History, and crossed-out cautious wording;
- localized red glitch marks instead of a full-page red wash; and
- truthful `SOURCE CHECK 0%` progress.

### Phase 1 repaired

- careful claim about blue/yellow versus red/green perception;
- limited-color spectrum graphic;
- claim-matched citations, source list, and active History;
- neutral `CLAIMS NEED SOURCES` treatment and restrained evidence summary; and
- `SOURCE CHECK 100%` plus completed reading metrics.

### Phase 2 super-corruption

- distinct AI override, not merely a return to the first screen;
- `JUST TRUST ME`, `AI CONFIDENCE 10,000%`, and `NO SOURCE NEEDED`;
- citations replaced with `AI SAYS SO`;
- History crossed out with a large X and replaced by `TRUST ME`;
- careful language forcibly rewritten as absolutes; and
- companion-only `LOCK IN THE FIX — 0 OF 3` objective.

## Techno overlay contract

Techno is a persistent desktop pet overlay and is not part of the webpage content.
Position and animation can change with gameplay while avoiding the reading text and
important site controls:

- initial corruption: `file-search` behavior while following missing citations;
- repaired state: `review-ready` lightbulb behavior; and
- super-corruption: `failed` or alarmed behavior while watching the AI override.

The broader animation sheet also supports idle, directional running, waiting,
working, file search, floppy-drive, USB delivery, and data-restored behaviors for
later implementation.
