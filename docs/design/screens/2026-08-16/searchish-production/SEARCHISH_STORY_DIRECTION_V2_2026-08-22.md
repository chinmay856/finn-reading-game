# Search-ish story direction v2

Status: current review candidate based on Chinmay's 2026-08-22 feedback. This is
not a frozen production design.

## Mission intent

Finn searches for a **print copy** of *Adventures of Huckleberry Finn*. Search
should help him reach the options that satisfy that request. An AI overview and
a paid result may remain useful parts of the page, but neither should replace
Finn's goal, hide the result list, disguise sponsorship, or choose for him.

This mission is about search hierarchy and user intent. It is not a second
WikiWhy mission about proving whether a factual claim is supported.

## Initial corruption

- Finn's exact print-copy query remains visible.
- A huge AI overview answers a different question: `YOU DON'T NEED THE BOOK. I
  READ IT. JUST ASK ME.`
- Internet Mega Bookstore occupies the next large block. Its `sponsored` label
  is tiny, while its monopoly and impossible-delivery claims dominate.
- The public-library and neighborhood-bookstore results remain technically
  present but are visually minimized below the dominant blocks.

## First repair run

The six visual repairs occur in this order:

1. Correct the AI overview. While still oversized, it identifies the novel,
   author, approximate publication period, and availability of print copies.
2. Shrink the corrected AI overview into optional help.
3. Make the paid-placement label unmistakable.
4. Restore the public-library result and availability information.
5. Restore the neighborhood-bookstore result and pickup information.
6. Rerank the complete page: library first, neighborhood bookstore second,
   clearly labeled paid result third, and optional AI help last.

The fully repaired page preserves all three acquisition options. Finn is not
required to select one.

## Midpoint and over-correction

Draft Chinmay intent, pending dialogue polish:

> I asked Otto to save you time by putting the most useful answer and the
> easiest way to get the book together at the top.

The instruction is plausibly helpful but leaves `most useful` and `easiest`
undefined. Otto interprets it as permission to finish the search for Finn:

- the search bar is disabled under `NO MORE SEARCHING REQUIRED`;
- the AI answer expands to almost the entire result surface;
- the Internet Mega Bookstore ad is embedded inside the AI answer as its
  recommended solution;
- the paid label becomes tiny again; and
- the public-library and neighborhood-bookstore results collapse into
  `OTHER OPTIONS (47)`.

## Lock-in order

1. `MAKE AI OPTIONAL`
2. `SHOW REAL OPTIONS`
3. `LABEL PAID RESULTS`
4. `KEEP FINN'S SEARCH`

The final secured page returns to the repaired hierarchy. Finn's later written
reflection supplies the lesson to Otto; the site itself does not require a
purchase or acquisition choice.

## Content note

Candidate Reading Companion passages may include carefully selected public-
domain excerpts, catalog records, and short original contextual passages. Any
excerpt containing sensitive historical language requires educator/content
review and context; public-domain status alone is not sufficient.
