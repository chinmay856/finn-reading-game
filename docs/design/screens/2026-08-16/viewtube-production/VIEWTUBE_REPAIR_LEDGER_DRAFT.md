# ViewTube repair ledger — first-round review draft

Status: review candidate, not a design freeze. The hobby-video title is an
editable example; the user-choice structure is the durable design target.

## Directed lesson

Recommendations, autoplay, and advertising incentives can conflict with the
video Finn actually wants. Search and the next-play decision remain with Finn.

## Phase-one repair sequence

The intended later expansion uses four passage-driven repairs, ordered from
smallest to largest visual change:

1. **Restore Finn's search.** His stop-motion hobby query appears in the search
   box while the forced queue remains corrupted.
2. **Label why videos appear.** `Searched for` and `Suggested` become distinct.
3. **Restore autoplay choice.** The toggle returns and the oversized forced queue
   collapses.
4. **Show ads and runtime.** Ad markers, total ads, and runtime become visible;
   the repaired bookend shows a hobby result selected by Finn and no autoplay.

Unresolved red wording stays fixed until the repair that replaces it. Repaired
wording stays fixed after that point.

## Auto over-fix

Chinmay's later dialogue says only that Auto can send Finn directly to the most
viral videos. Auto's own status reveals the hidden objective:

- `OPTIMIZING FOR THE MOST ADS`
- `AUTOPLAYING PARTS 1–47`
- `SEARCH INTENT REMOVED`

The queue escalates into ads about ads while Auto remains earnestly procedural.

## Lock-in checklist

1. `SEARCH WORKS`
2. `SHOW WHY THIS IS NEXT`
3. `ASK BEFORE PLAYING`
4. `SELECTED BY FINN`

The lock overlay is green; its unresolved items remain canonical red until the
later lock-in sequence is authored.
