# MyCorner repair ledger — review draft

Status: first-round anchor logic for Chinmay review. Dialogue and exact player-facing copy remain editable.

## Lesson

A copied picture and polished profile do not prove who controls an account. When an online profile makes an urgent money request, verify the person through a known route that the request does not control.

## Initial repair sequence

Ordered from small metadata changes to the largest identity comparison:

1. **Restore Amy's known profile details.** Place stable biographical details beside the copy.
2. **Show the new-account history.** Reveal that the impersonator profile appeared minutes ago.
3. **Mark the urgent promise.** Surface the stranded-abroad story and promised return as claims from the unverified account.
4. **Expose timeline contradictions.** Show mutually impossible locations and changing details.
5. **Verify another way.** Restore Amy's known contact route and the fully repaired comparison.

## Auto over-fix

Auto mistakes image polish for identity proof. The paper-mask profile receives a royal airport background, a 4K badge, seventeen generated proof photos, and `AI VERIFIED 9000%`. The urgent request becomes even more confident.

## Final lock-in repairs

1. **Photo is not identity**
2. **Check the timeline**
3. **Verify another way**
4. **Do not send money**

The secured state retains Amy's known profile and labels the impersonator as unverified/blocked. No payment control or real transaction exists.
