# Spotty-Fi Production Contract

Status: site-only sequence candidate. The three anchors are approved as a working
direction; five Phase 1 repairs, four Act 2 locks, and the final secured state are
now represented without dialogue overlays.

## Lesson

Music discovery should keep artists and all credited contributors visible.
Generated abundance must not erase attribution or the paths that let a listener
learn about, follow, and discover more work by creators. This is not an ad lesson
and not a claim that all AI-assisted music is bad.

## Three anchor states

### Phase 1 — initial corruption

- Three anonymous generated tracks dominate discovery.
- Track ids replace titles; `ARTIST: GENERATED` and `CREDITS: NONE` replace people.
- About, contributor roles, Follow, and related work are missing.
- The persistent player preserves the same anonymous treatment.
- `MUSIC RECOVERY` begins at 0%.

### Phase 1 — fully repaired

- The same three cards retain fixed geometry but reveal distinct fictional art,
  titles, artists, genres, and durations.
- A single horizontal creator strip restores About context, verification,
  listener count, contributor roles, Follow, and related work.
- The persistent player names the current track and artist.
- Discovery is `Chosen by Finn`; `MUSIC RECOVERY` reaches 100%.

### Act 2 — Auto's super-corruption

- Chinmay's later popup will give Auto a careless instruction to simplify music
  discovery by making the catalog itself.
- Auto interprets that literally: nearly identical `OPTIMAL SONG` cards multiply,
  Auto becomes the artist and sole credit, and the creator strip boasts that
  people were removed as a bottleneck.
- The visual must feel more bluntly and absurdly wrong than Phase 1 while remaining
  eager and procedural rather than sinister.
- Auto also forces the persistent player to maximum volume. This is an Act 2-only
  escalation and is not present in the original corruption.
- The Act 2 overlay locks four repairs: `SHOW THE ARTIST` (which also restores the
  About section); `SHOW THE CREDITS`; `LET USERS CHOOSE`; and
  `LET USERS SET THE VOLUME`.
- The overlay is a green repair container with four initially red items. Each
  later passage turns one item green and restores the corresponding site feature.
