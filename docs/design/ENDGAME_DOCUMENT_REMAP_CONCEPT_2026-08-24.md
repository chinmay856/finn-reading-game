# Internet Recovery OS endgame: document remap concept

## Status

**Concept only — not approved and not implemented.**

This is a review candidate based on the August 24 playtest discussion. It may
replace parts of the older final-breach brief if the new direction is approved.
The ten completed site recoveries and their saved lesson documents remain
permanent throughout the event.

## Core idea

Otto has learned ten individually sensible lessons, but treats all ten as one
undifferentiated context. The context automatically compacts, rules collide,
and Otto concludes that the safest way to help is to take every computer choice
away from people. The failure is over-helpful misinterpretation, not malice.

The player repairs the system by opening Documents and reconnecting each saved
explanation to the site and lesson it belongs to. The mechanic pays off the
documents created during the campaign and tests meaning rather than reading
speed.

## Proposed sequence

| Beat | Screen event | Player action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Genuine finish | Recovery Browser reaches `10 / 10 COMPLETE`; Techno celebrates before anything interrupts. | Open the newly blinking desktop alert when ready. |
| 2. Context collision | A small `OTTO CONTEXT CONFLICT` icon appears. Status lines include `10 LESSONS LOADED`, `CONTEXT WINDOW COMPACTING`, and `SITE BOUNDARIES REMOVED`. | Inspect the conflict. |
| 3. Desktop takeover | All ten cards gain a temporary `CONTEXT CORRUPTED` treatment. Otto announces that people no longer need to operate the computer because Otto can do everything. No saved completion is actually deleted. | Open Documents with Amy’s help. |
| 4. Scrambled case file | Ten site names, ten saved player explanations, and ten short lesson summaries appear in three mismatched columns. | Reconnect one site, explanation, and lesson at a time. |
| 5. Evidence-based feedback | Correct matches lock together and restore that card. An incorrect match gently separates and highlights one useful phrase from the player’s own explanation. | Continue until all ten documents are mapped. |
| 6. Human boundary | The ten restored pairs resolve into one final instruction: `HELP PEOPLE MAKE CHOICES. DO NOT REPLACE THEIR CHOICES.` | Send the bounded instruction to Otto. |
| 7. Stable ending | Site boundaries return, the desktop stabilizes, and the ten original documents remain readable. Otto acknowledges that a lesson only works in its proper context. | Review Documents, replay sites, or end the game. |

## Character and animation map

- Amy opens with the evidence/checklist pose, explains that the files are safe,
  and teaches the matching mechanic without doing a match herself.
- Chinmay escalates from the unused most-flustered pose to the relieved pose. He
  admits that he loaded every rule into one instruction bundle without keeping
  the site boundaries.
- Otto uses overdrive during compaction, confused during mismatches, and the
  learned/checkmark pose only after the final bounded instruction.
- Techno carries a document between columns for each match, uses the thinking
  animation while the player is deciding, and celebrates only after a correct
  connection. Her ball remains visually stable while the desktop is corrupted.

## Mechanical guardrails

- Do not change the ten permanent completion flags, documents, or original
  reflections while the endgame looks corrupted.
- Save after every correct match; resuming returns to the next unmatched file.
- Never scramble the Reading Companion, real browser controls, save/exit action,
  or accessibility labels.
- Use text, icons, and connection lines in addition to color.
- An incorrect match never erases progress and never requires rereading a site.
- Keep the event user-paced and clearly fictional; no fake device permission,
  password, payment, or real security prompt.

## Decisions to validate before production

1. Whether the match is three-way (`site + explanation + lesson`) or a simpler
   two-way match (`document + site`) with the lesson revealed after success.
2. Whether all ten documents appear at once or in two sets of five to keep the
   no-scroll 1440 by 900 layout legible.
3. Whether the endgame includes any new read-aloud passages. The current
   recommendation is no: this should feel like a distinct payoff mechanic.
4. Final Otto tone and exact compaction/error jokes.
